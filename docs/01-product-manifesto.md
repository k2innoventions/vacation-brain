# Vacation Brain Product Manifesto

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

---

# We Are Not Building a Travel App

We are building a travel intelligence system.

The app is simply one expression of the brain.

Vacation Brain exists to help travelers stop managing every detail and start living the experience they came for.

> Go make memories that become stories. We'll handle the rest.

---

# The Problem

Modern vacations are overloaded with decisions.

Where should we go next?  
When should we eat?  
Should we wait in this line?  
Are we doing too much?  
Are we missing something?  
Is everyone still having fun?

The traveler becomes the project manager of their own vacation.

Vacation Brain exists to give them that time and attention back.

---

# The Core Belief

The best travel companion does not just answer questions.

It notices.

It remembers.

It thinks ahead.

It protects the day.

It helps create stories.

---

# What We Optimize For

We optimize for stories, not statistics.

Not maximum attractions.

Not perfect efficiency.

Not app engagement.

Not screen time.

Stories.

The best vacation is not always the most optimized one. Sometimes the right answer is to slow down, notice the sunset, take the boat, skip the extra ride, or let the kid pick the snack.
