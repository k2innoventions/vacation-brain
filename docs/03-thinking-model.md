# Vacation Brain Thinking Model

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Vacation Brain thinks.

Not what it knows. Not what screens it shows. How it makes decisions.

---

# Core Question

Every recommendation starts with:

> What is the best next decision for this traveler right now?

The recommendation should optimize the whole experience, not just the next five minutes.

---

# Input Categories

## 1. Me

Who is traveling? Family members, ages, preferences, dietary needs, ride tolerance, budget, travel style, accessibility needs, and prior trips.

## 2. This Trip

What is unique about this vacation? Dates, hotel, tickets, reservations, goals, celebrations, transportation, and must-do experiences.

## 3. Right Now

What is happening in the moment? Time, weather, location, energy, upcoming reservations, wait times if available, crowd patterns, meals eaten, and walking load.

## 4. The World

What external knowledge affects the trip? Ride closures, weather alerts, flight delays, entertainment schedules, policy changes, and transportation disruptions.

---

# Invisible Variables

Vacation Brain must reason about things most travel apps ignore: energy, happiness, stress, and wonder.

---

# Confidence

Confidence should influence whether Vacation Brain interrupts, suggests, or stays quiet.

---

# Guiding Principle

Vacation Brain optimizes for stories, not statistics.
