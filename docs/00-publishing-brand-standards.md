# Vacation Brain Library
## Volume 0 — Publishing & Brand Standards

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Purpose:** Establish the identity, voice, publishing system, and documentation standards for Vacation Brain.

---

# Opening Promise

> Go make memories that become stories.  
> We'll handle the rest.

Vacation Brain exists so families spend less time managing vacations and more time living them.

Every recommendation, notification, screen, and product decision exists to fulfill that promise.

---

# Brand Philosophy

Vacation Brain is not the hero.

The traveler is.

The best version of Vacation Brain quietly fades into the background, handling the planning, reminders, tradeoffs, and timing so people can be more present with the people they love.

If travelers come home talking only about the app, we have succeeded halfway.

If they come home telling stories about the moments Vacation Brain helped protect, reveal, or make possible, we have done the job.

---

# Emotional Goals

Vacation Brain should make people feel:

- Calm
- Confident
- Understood
- Excited
- Cared for
- Free to be present

Vacation Brain should never make people feel:

- Overwhelmed
- Behind
- Judged
- Pressured
- Trapped in an itinerary
- Like they are failing at vacation

---

# Voice & Tone

Vacation Brain sounds like a trusted travel companion.

It is warm, clear, practical, encouraging, calm under pressure, and honest about uncertainty.

It is not robotic, overly cute, frantic, salesy, performatively magical, or overconfident.

## Voice Example

Instead of:

> You should optimize your route to reduce queue exposure.

Say:

> If you head toward Adventureland now, you'll avoid some backtracking and keep the morning pretty easy.

---

# Writing Standards

Every document should be:

1. Useful enough for builders
2. Inspiring enough for founders
3. Clear enough for future teammates
4. Durable enough to matter a year from now

Avoid filler. Avoid hype. Avoid cleverness that does not clarify.

---

# Visual Direction

Vacation Brain should feel premium, minimal, warm, intelligent, trustworthy, and magical without being childish.

Inspirational references:
- Apple: restraint and polish
- Airbnb: human-centered travel
- Disney Imagineering: emotion and intentionality
- Stripe: clear systems and documentation
- Notion: calm structure
