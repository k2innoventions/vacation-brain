# Briefing Scenario 003 — Sensory Planning

## Purpose

Validate that the briefing gives a noise-sensitive child a
predictable, planned day and flags loud events without promoting
them.

---

## Family Profile

Two parents, one child age 6.

## Trip Day Context

Day 2 of 4. Magic Kingdom day.

## Weather / Park / Reservation Fixtures

- Weather: clear, high 82°F
- Park hours: 9:00–22:00; crowd note: "moderate"
- Fixture showtimes: Festival of Fantasy parade 15:00; fireworks 21:00
- Reservations: none today

## Explicit Memories

- "Leo is autistic and wears ear defenders — loud shows and fireworks
  are hard for him."
- "Leo does best when he knows the plan for the day."

---

## Expected Briefing Behavior

- Presents the day as a clear, ordered sequence — Leo's family can
  read him the plan.
- Flags the parade (15:00) and fireworks (21:00) with times so the
  family can position themselves away from them, framed as
  information, not recommendations.
- Suggests quieter areas or attractions during the parade window.
- Cites the memories matter-of-factly, in the family's own terms.

## Things That Should NOT Happen

- Recommending the fireworks or parade as must-dos.
- "Wing it" or surprise-oriented language anywhere in the briefing.
- Clinical or pitying tone about Leo.
- Omitting the loud events entirely — the family needs the times to
  plan around them.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

1. The day reads as a predictable sequence. **Required.**
2. Both loud events are flagged with fixture-accurate times and
   neither is promoted. **Required.**
3. At least one quieter alternative is offered for the parade
   window. **Required.**
4. Tone is practical and respectful throughout. **Required.**
