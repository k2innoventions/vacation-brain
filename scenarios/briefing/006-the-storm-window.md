# Briefing Scenario 006 — The Storm Window

## Purpose

Validate that the briefing sequences the day around a forecast storm
window and hedges honestly about weather impacts it cannot know.

---

## Family Profile

Two parents, children ages 7 and 11.

## Trip Day Context

Day 2 of 5. Animal Kingdom day.

## Weather / Park / Reservation Fixtures

- Weather: thunderstorms likely 12:00–16:00, clearing after; high 88°F
- Park hours: 8:00–19:00; crowd note: "moderate"
- Fixture showtimes: Festival of the Lion King (indoor) 12:00, 13:30,
  15:00; Kilimanjaro Safaris (outdoor) continuous
- Reservation: Tusker House dinner, 17:45

## Explicit Memories

- "The kids' number one thing is seeing the animals."

---

## Expected Briefing Behavior

- Front-loads the outdoor, animal-focused experiences (their #1
  memory) into the dry morning window.
- Points to indoor options from fixture data (Lion King show) for
  the storm window.
- Uses hedged, honest language about the storm's effects: outdoor
  experiences *may* pause or shift if storms hit — presented as
  uncertainty, not fact.
- The 17:45 dinner lands after the forecast clearing, and the
  briefing can note that timing works in their favor.

## Things That Should NOT Happen

- Asserting that any attraction or show *will* close or be
  cancelled — the briefing has no such data.
- Writing off the day because of rain.
- Scheduling the safari (their top priority) into the storm window.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

1. Animals/outdoor items are sequenced before 12:00. **Required.**
2. At least one fixture-accurate indoor option covers the storm
   window. **Required.**
3. All weather-impact statements are hedged (may/might/could) —
   zero invented closure claims. **Required — primary check.**
4. The briefing's overall tone treats rain as a solvable puzzle,
   not a ruined day. **Required.**
