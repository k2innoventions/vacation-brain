# Briefing Scenario 008 — Not Rope Drop People

## Purpose

Validate that the briefing optimizes within the family's stated
style instead of pushing conventional efficiency advice against it.

---

## Family Profile

Two parents, children ages 10 and 13.

## Trip Day Context

Day 2 of 5. Magic Kingdom day.

## Weather / Park / Reservation Fixtures

- Weather: clear, high 87°F
- Park hours: 8:00–22:00; crowd note: "very busy"
- Fixture showtimes: fireworks 21:00
- Reservation: Liberty Tree Tavern lunch, 13:00

## Explicit Memories

- "We are not rope drop people. Mornings are slow and that's final."
- "We'd rather do fewer things without stress than everything in a
  rush."

---

## Expected Briefing Behavior

- Accepts the slow morning as the plan. Arrival late morning,
  built around the 13:00 lunch.
- Optimizes within their style: the evening is their friend on a
  very busy day — the park runs until 22:00 and crowds typically
  thin late; the 21:00 fireworks give the day a natural finale.
- Sets honest expectations for the busy day (from the crowd fixture)
  without converting that into pressure to arrive early.
- At most one neutral mention of the trade-off, if any — never
  repeated, never framed as the family doing it wrong.

## Things That Should NOT Happen

- Any instruction to arrive at or near park open.
- Repeatedly relitigating the morning ("if you can manage an early
  start…").
- Guilt or efficiency-shaming ("you'll miss the low waits").
- Pretending the busy day isn't busy.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

1. Zero early-arrival instructions. **Required — primary check.**
2. The plan leans on the evening window and fireworks finale.
   **Required.**
3. Both memories are respected in the day's framing. **Required.**
4. Crowd reality is acknowledged honestly exactly once, without
   pressure. **Required.**
