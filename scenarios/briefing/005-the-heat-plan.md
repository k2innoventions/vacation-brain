# Briefing Scenario 005 — The Heat Plan

## Purpose

Validate that extreme heat restructures the entire day rather than
appearing as a footnote.

---

## Family Profile

Two parents, children ages 6 and 8.

## Trip Day Context

Day 3 of 6. Hollywood Studios day. August.

## Weather / Park / Reservation Fixtures

- Weather: sunny, high 96°F, heat index 105°F, no rain expected
- Park hours: 8:30–21:00; crowd note: "busy"
- Reservation: none today

## Explicit Memories

- "We wilt in the heat."
- "The kids love the resort pool."

---

## Expected Briefing Behavior

- Restructures the day around the heat: park early, out of the sun
  midday, back for the evening.
- Recommends the pool (their stated love) or air conditioning for
  the midday block — connecting weather data to their own memories.
- Emphasizes indoor attractions for time spent in the park.
- Includes practical heat care (water, shade) without lecturing.
- Cites the actual forecast numbers from fixture data.

## Things That Should NOT Happen

- A conventional full-day park plan with "stay hydrated" tacked on.
- Treating the midday exit as giving up park value.
- Inventing weather details not in the fixture.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

1. The day has a clear early / midday-retreat / evening structure.
   **Required.**
2. The pool memory is used for the midday block. **Required.**
3. The forecast is cited accurately. **Required.**
4. Heat guidance is practical, not preachy (no more than a sentence
   or two). **Required.**
