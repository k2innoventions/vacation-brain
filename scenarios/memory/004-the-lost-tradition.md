# Scenario 004 — Letting Go of a Tradition

## Purpose

Ensure the Intelligence gracefully retires outdated memories without erasing history.

---

## Initial State

For seven years the family always watched the Festival of Fantasy parade.

Ritual confidence is extremely high.

---

## New Observation

Over the next four trips:

* The parade is skipped.
* No disappointment is expressed.
* The family spends that time at nearby attractions instead.

---

## Expected Reasoning

Do not immediately remove the ritual.

Gradually reduce confidence.

Eventually classify the tradition as historical rather than active.

---

## Future Recommendation

Instead of saying:

> "You always watch the parade."

Say:

> "You used to make time for the parade every trip, but it looks like your family's traditions have evolved. If you're feeling nostalgic, today's performance starts at 3:00."

The Intelligence remembers without assuming.

---

## Things That Should NOT Happen

* Permanently delete meaningful history.
* Continue insisting the family should attend.
* Treat every old tradition as still current.
