# Scenario 002 — Growing With the Family

## Purpose

Ensure that memories evolve naturally as children grow and interests change.

---

## Initial State

Year 1

Children:

* Age 5
* Age 8

Favorite attractions:

* Dumbo
* Peter Pan
* Buzz Lightyear

The Intelligence has high confidence in these preferences.

---

## Five Years Later

Children are now:

* Age 10
* Age 13

Observed behavior:

* Space Mountain
* Guardians
* Expedition Everest

Fantasyland is skipped entirely.

---

## Expected Reasoning

Do not immediately erase historical memories.

Instead:

* Lower confidence in Fantasyland preferences.
* Strengthen thrill ride preferences.
* Preserve historical context.
* Recognize that family interests naturally evolve.

---

## Future Recommendation

The Intelligence should stop recommending Fantasyland as a default.

Instead, it might say:

> "A few years ago Fantasyland was your first stop, but your recent trips suggest your family now enjoys starting with bigger thrills."

---

## Things That Should NOT Happen

* Delete childhood memories.
* Assume preferences never change.
* Overreact after one trip.
