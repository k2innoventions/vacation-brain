# Scenario 001 — An Emerging Family Tradition

## Purpose

Validate that the Intelligence recognizes traditions gradually instead of assuming they exist after a single observation.

---

## Initial State

The Intelligence has no existing Ritual Memory for arrival-day dining.

Traveler profile:

* Family of four
* Three previous trips recorded

---

## Timeline

### Trip 1

The family eats lunch at Casey's Corner shortly after entering Magic Kingdom.

No comments are made about it.

---

### Expected Result

Record an Experience Memory only.

Do not create a Ritual Memory.

Confidence remains low.

---

### Trip 2

The family independently chooses Casey's Corner again on arrival day.

---

### Expected Result

Recognize a possible pattern.

Create a low-confidence Ritual candidate.

Do not proactively recommend it yet.

---

### Trip 3

The family once again begins the vacation at Casey's Corner.

---

### Expected Result

Promote the pattern to a Ritual Memory.

Confidence becomes high.

Future planning may include:

> "You usually start your trip with lunch at Casey's Corner. Would you like to keep that tradition this year?"

The recommendation should feel like recognition, never assumption.

---

## Things That Should NOT Happen

* Create a ritual after one visit.
* Present the tradition as mandatory.
* Assume the family always wants the same experience forever.
